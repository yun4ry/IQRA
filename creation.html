Our Purpose and Creation in Islam

1. Why Were We Created?

Allah clearly states in the Qur’an: “And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me” (51:56). Our purpose is to know, love, and worship our Creator.

2. We Need Allah — He Doesn’t Need Us

Allah is Al-Ghani (The Self-Sufficient). He has no need for us, our worship, or our existence. We, on the other hand, are in constant need of His mercy, guidance, and sustenance (Qur’an 14:8).

3. Everything is Created for a Reason

Nothing in this life is random. Every soul, trial, and blessing is part of a greater plan. Reflecting on creation increases our faith in Allah’s wisdom and justice.
