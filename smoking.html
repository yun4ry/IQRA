Why Is Smoking Prohibited?

1. Health Risks

Smoking is one of the leading causes of preventable diseases like cancer, heart disease, and respiratory issues.

2. Self-Harm is Forbidden

Islam strictly forbids actions that harm the body — smoking falls under this due to its proven long-term health effects.

3. Financial & Social Harm

Smoking wastes wealth and can harm others through secondhand smoke, which goes against Islamic principles of responsibility.
