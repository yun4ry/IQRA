Who Are We?

1. A Team of Muslim Computer Scientists

We’re a group of Muslim developers, designers, and tech lovers who want to use our skills to make Islamic knowledge more accessible and beautifully presented online.

2. Qur’an Meets Code

In a world filled with distractions, we aim to bring people back to the truth — through interactive websites, clean UI, and thoughtful design centered on the Qur’an.

3. No Pressure. Just Clarity.

This isn’t da’wah with guilt — it’s da’wah with sincerity. Whether you’re Muslim or just curious, we hope you find something here that makes you pause and reflect.
