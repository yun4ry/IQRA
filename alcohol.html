Why Is Drinking Alcohol Prohibited?

1. Mental Impairment

Alcohol affects judgment, behavior, and self-control — all of which are central to Islamic values of responsibility.

2. Harm to Body and Soul

Islam forbids substances that damage the body or cloud the mind. Alcohol is both physically and spiritually harmful.

3. Social Consequences

Alcohol often leads to broken families, violence, and addiction — all of which contradict Islam’s goals of peace and stability.
