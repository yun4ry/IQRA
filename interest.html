The Concept of Interest (Riba) in Islam

1. Riba is Strictly Prohibited

The Qur’an clearly prohibits the taking or giving of interest (riba), considering it unjust and exploitative. “Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest…” (Qur’an 2:275).

2. Interest vs Trade

Islam differentiates between fair trade and interest. Trade involves risk and value exchange, while riba is guaranteed profit with no shared risk, making it unfair to the borrower.

3. Ethical Financial Dealings

Islam encourages financial justice — fairness, transparency, and support for the needy. Interest-based systems can lead to wealth inequality and social harm.
