What Does Islam Say About Other Religions?

Islam acknowledges that Allah sent messengers and revealed scriptures to many nations throughout history. The Torah and the Gospel were once divine revelations. But the truth is — over time, people changed those books. They edited, erased, and reworded what Allah originally sent down.

This is why the Qur’an was revealed as the final message — perfectly preserved by Allah and protected from corruption. The Qur’an confirms what was true in previous scriptures, but corrects the distortions and replaces outdated laws.

“They distort words from their [proper] usages and have forgotten a portion of that of which they were reminded.” — Qur’an 5:13

So while Islam teaches respect and kindness toward people of other religions, it also makes it clear: continuing to follow altered scriptures today is rejecting Allah’s final word. The only truth that remains untouched is the Qur’an — the complete and final guidance for humanity.

Those who now hear the message of Islam but choose man-made religion instead of Allah’s command — they are accountable. The truth has come. What’s left is our response.
