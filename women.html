Are Women Oppressed in Islam?

No, Islam does not oppress women — it honors them. From the very beginning, Allah elevated the status of women spiritually, intellectually, and socially. Any oppression seen today is due to culture or ignorance, not Islam itself.

Women in Islam have the right to education, to work, to own property, to inherit, to choose their spouse, and to be treated with dignity and respect. These rights were given by Allah centuries before modern societies even considered them.

“Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you.” — Qur’an 49:13

Allah doesn't judge people by gender — He judges by taqwa (God-consciousness). The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said, "The best of you are those who are best to their women." He uplifted daughters, honored wives, and encouraged kindness toward mothers.

Oppression is never from Islam. It’s against everything the Qur’an stands for. Islam empowered women long before the world recognized their worth — and continues to do so through divine guidance, not human bias.
