The Value of Mercy in Islam

1. Allah is the Most Merciful

The most repeated names of Allah in the Qur’an are Ar-Rahman and Ar-Raheem — the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. His mercy encompasses all things (7:156).

2. The Prophet's ﷺ Mercy

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was described as a mercy to the worlds (Qur’an 21:107). He showed compassion to children, animals, and even enemies.

3. Mercy Among People

Islam encourages believers to show mercy to others. “He who does not show mercy to others will not be shown mercy” (Hadith, Bukhari).
