What is Islam?

1. Islam Means Peace & Submission

Islam comes from the word “Salaam,” which means peace. It also means submitting your will to Allah, the Creator of everything.

2. One God. One Message.

Muslims believe in One God — Allah — and follow the teachings of the final Prophet, Muhammad ﷺ. The same message was sent through prophets like Moses and Jesus (peace be upon them).

3. Not Just a Religion — A Way of Life

Islam isn’t just about rituals. It’s about how you treat others, how you connect with God, and how you live with purpose every day.
